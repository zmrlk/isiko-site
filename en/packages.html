Frequently asked.

How much does it cost?
Every company is a different case. A 7-day audit for a 20-person company is a different situation than for a 300-person one. A 1 day/week retainer is a different intensity than 2 days. We agree it together on the first call — once I see what we're dealing with. There are no hidden costs, no „onboarding fee", no rigid price list to sign.
Why no packaged 30/60/90 day sprints?
The reality of companies with 1–300 people doesn't fit neatly into sprints. Processes change gradually, AI requires iteration, people need time to get used to new tools. A weekly cadence with an Operations Director / CTO embedded in the team delivers more value than a packaged sprint that ends with a PDF.
Can I buy AI alone, without an audit?
Yes — the AI workshop for the board (1 day), team training (1–3 days) and AI implementation (per use case) are standalone. But: in 90% of cases AI without a prior process audit = automation deployed into chaos. The 7-day audit is not an obligation — it's a saving.
What if our problem is something else?
Write to us. I'll recommend a direction even if I'm not the right person. I have a network of other consultants (processes, finance, HR, legal) we work with. Better to refer than to take an engagement outside my competence.
VAT invoice?
Yes — registered business activity, VAT invoices, monthly settlement. Engagements formalised under a framework agreement.
