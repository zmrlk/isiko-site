METHOD · three steps

Diagnose. Implement.
Hand over.

Always three steps. Always in the same order. Boring. Repeatable. It works.

01 · circle

diagnosis.

First we look. We sit with the team, with documents and processes. We observe how the company actually works — before anyone tells us where it hurts. Because once you listen to one person first, you stop seeing the whole. Only then do we name the problem. You leave with a map: what we fix in a month, what in a quarter, what stays.

02 · arc

implementation.

We connect things. Embedded in the team's rhythm — we do whatever is needed, every step documented in plain language with the reasoning. We pay close attention to UX and to the experience of the people using the system — a system nobody uses is nothing. The client reads on Monday and understands what we're doing on Wednesday.

03 · isiko

handover.

In the end, what stays is a system that runs itself. Step-by-step instructions, a record of decisions, a trained team. For 2-3 months after launch we pick up the phone. Then your team carries on. And we go looking for the next company where something hurts.
